# Quality And Submission

## Why

The final delivery should explain what was improved, why priorities were chosen and how AI was used. This matters because the challenge explicitly asks for tradeoffs and documentation.

## How

Finalize commands, README and submission notes after the core behavior is implemented and verified.

Keep the explanation concise and focused on decisions: architecture, Docker-first environment, persistence, validation, tests, security measures and remaining tradeoffs.
